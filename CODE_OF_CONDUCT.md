# Code of Conduct

## Standard

Be civil, be specific, and bring evidence.

This project's technical culture is adversarial toward *claims* and never toward
*people*. Demonstrating that a result is wrong — including one of the
maintainer's — is the single most valuable contribution available here. Four
such findings are recorded in [README §14](README.md#14-what-we-got-wrong).
Report them plainly; they will be published.

## Expected

- Critique the measurement, the control, or the argument.
- State what a claim was compared against.
- Report negative results as readily as positive ones.

## Unacceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **teerthsharma@outlook.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or
contributions that violate this document, and may ban contributors for repeated
or severe violations.
